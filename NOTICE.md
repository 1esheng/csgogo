## English

**The copyright of every player photograph in this project belongs to the original sites, photographers and their rightful owners.**

- Sources: **prosettings.gg**, **prosettings.net**, **profilerr.net**, **Wikimedia Commons**, **Wikipedia** (their pages and the respective uploaders / photographers)
- This project **claims no copyright** over any photograph and has **no commercial licence** for them
- Purpose: **personal study, research and free sharing only** (the code is MIT-licensed and contains no paid content)
- Per-image provenance is recorded in `src/assets/avatars/credits.json` (original file names), and the card UI shows “图源 / source: xxx”
- Where no photo exists, the game renders a **procedurally generated silhouette avatar** instead of any third-party asset

### Takedown

**If you are a rights holder and believe any image here is used improperly, please open an Issue in this repository (or contact the maintainer directly). I will remove the image immediately after verification — no legal process required.**

We respect the work of every photographer and site; this project does not intend to replace, copy, or divert traffic or content from any commercial site.
